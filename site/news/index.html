You can now support Folkweb

14 October 2026

Folkweb stays free and complete for everyone. If you’d like to help it reach the browser stores and keep going, you can now donate through GitHub Sponsors, Ko-fi or Liberapay. Donations are gifts: no perks, and no say over the lists. Every year we’ll publish what came in and what it paid for. The privacy policy now explains what we see when you donate. How to support Folkweb.

Folkweb is being built

12 October 2026

Folkweb is a free browser extension that shows signs of the people behind what you read, lets you choose whose judgement to trust, and quiets the noise. It is in testing now. This page and the feed below will announce the launch for Firefox, Edge and Chrome.

Follow with RSS

RSS lets you follow sites in a feed reader of your choice, with no account, algorithm or tracking. Copy this address into your reader:

https://folkweb.org/feed.xml

New to RSS? Readers include NetNewsWire (Mac, iPhone), Feeder (Android), Thunderbird and Inoreader.
