How lists are curated

Folkweb exists to put people back in charge. Our lists have to earn trust, so these rules apply to every entry.

  1. Evidence, not vibes. Every block-list addition links public, reproducible evidence, such as archived pages showing mass-generated content, fabricated authors, or recycled text across many pages. “Feels like AI” is not evidence.
  2. Judge content, not people. No entries based on politics, identity, competition or personal disputes.
  3. Boost means human-run. A boost entry is a site primarily made and maintained by people, with a note on who vouches for it and why.
  4. Removal is equal to addition. Site owners can request removal. Requests are reviewed with the same weight and timeline.
  5. No secret lists. Every bundled entry is public and has a history.
  6. AI may draft, people decide. AI tools can propose changes and check filters, but a person approves every change.
  7. When in doubt, leave it out.

Money never buys a place

No site can pay to be boosted, or to be removed from a block list. Donations and grants never influence lists.

Lists are yours to change

You decide how the lists are used. In Folkweb’s settings you can turn off the ★ marks, the hiding of blocked results and the stop page, add your own boosted and blocked sites, or pause Folkweb on any site. The lists are published under CC BY-SA 4.0, so other tools can reuse them.

Propose a change or ask for a removal

From the public launch, use the issue forms in the project’s repository. Until then, email hello@folkweb.org with the domain and your evidence or reason.
