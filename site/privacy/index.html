Privacy

Short version: Folkweb collects nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics and no Folkweb servers that see your browsing.

Last updated 12 October 2026.

The browser extension

WhatWhere it goes
Your settings, boost and block lists, trust circleStored only in your browser (storage.local). Never sent anywhere. Removing the extension deletes them.
The pages you visitRead inside your browser to hide clutter, mark search results and apply themes. Nothing about them is sent to us or anyone else.
A site’s own human.jsonOnly when you open Folkweb’s toolbar popup on a page that links one on the same site. Folkweb then fetches it from that site, without cookies. The site sees an ordinary request, as when you load an image.
Content Credentials checksOnly when you right-click an image and ask. Folkweb asks permission for that site, then downloads the file from where it already is, without cookies, and reads it on your device. The reader never contacts any other address, even if the file names one.
Usage data, crash reports, analyticsNone. Folkweb has no telemetry of any kind. Firefox’s listing declares “no data collection”.

Folkweb contains no AI models and sends nothing to AI services.

This website

folkweb.org sets no cookies and runs no JavaScript, analytics or third-party embeds. It is hosted on Cloudflare, which, like any web host, processes your IP address and request details to deliver pages and stop attacks. We don’t receive or keep visitor logs.

Donations

If you donate, the platform you choose (GitHub Sponsors, Ko-fi or Liberapay) handles the payment under its own privacy policy. We only see what that platform shows recipients: a name or username, the amount and any message, and on Ko-fi also your email address. We use it only to say thank you, never publish donor names without asking, never send newsletters, and never add donors to any list. On Liberapay you can donate anonymously, even to us.

Email

If you write to us, we use your message only to reply. Mail to folkweb.org addresses is forwarded by Cloudflare Email Routing to the maintainer’s inbox.

Questions

Email hello@folkweb.org. If this policy changes, the change and its date will appear here and in the news.
