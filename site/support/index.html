Support Folkweb

Folkweb is free and complete for everyone, and will stay that way. No ads, no paid features, no data sales, no sponsored list entries.

Donations

Donation options (GitHub Sponsors and Ko-fi) open with the public launch. Money goes to keeping Folkweb running: the domain, store fees, the maintainer’s time, and, when possible, an independent security review.

Once donations start, we’ll publish a yearly summary of income and costs here. Donations never influence what goes on the lists.

Other ways to help

  • Publish a human.json on your own site and vouch for sites you know are made by people.
  • Tell us when a filter breaks: when an AI widget or ad slips through, or something useful gets hidden.
  • Suggest human-made sites to boost, with evidence (see how lists are curated).
  • Tell a friend who misses the human web.
