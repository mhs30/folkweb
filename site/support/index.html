Support Folkweb

Folkweb is free and complete for everyone, and will stay that way. No ads, no paid features, no data sales, no sponsored list entries.

Donate

Folkweb is made in spare time and is getting ready for the browser stores. If you’d like to help it get there and keep going, pick whichever platform suits you:

  • Ko-fi: a one-time tip by card or PayPal, no account needed.
  • Liberapay: weekly, monthly or yearly, run by a non-profit. You can stay anonymous, even to us.
  • GitHub Sponsors will follow soon.

Money goes to what keeps Folkweb running: the domain, browser store fees, an independent security review, and the maintainer’s time.

How money is handled

  • Free and complete for everyone. No paid features, ads, affiliate links or data sales, now or later.
  • Donations are gifts. There are no rewards or perks, and donors get no say over the lists, what gets hidden, or the code.
  • No site can pay to be boosted or to be removed from a block list. See how lists are curated.
  • No nagging. The extension mentions support in one place only: a link at the bottom of its Settings.
  • Grants from public-interest funds follow the same rules.

Income and costs

Every year we publish a short summary here: what came in, by platform and grant, and what it was spent on. The first summary will cover 2026.

Other ways to help

  • Publish a human.json on your own site and vouch for sites you know are made by people.
  • Tell us when a filter breaks: when an AI widget or ad slips through, or something useful gets hidden.
  • Suggest human-made sites to boost, with evidence (see how lists are curated).
  • Tell a friend who misses the human web.
