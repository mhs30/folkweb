The web, by folks.

Folkweb is a free browser extension that puts people back at the centre of the web. It shows you signs of the people behind what you read, lets you choose whose judgement to trust, and quiets the noise in between.

Folkweb isn’t against AI. It’s for people. We want AI that helps you find, understand and connect with other people’s work, never AI that replaces them or decides for you. You choose which AI you see.

Coming soon. Folkweb is being tested now and will be available for Firefox, Edge and Chrome. Follow the news (there’s an RSS feed) to hear when it launches.

What Folkweb does

  • 🧹 Hides the AI panels, AI summaries and sponsored modules you didn’t ask for, each one your choice, and shows you what it hid on each page and why, so you can bring it back.
  • 🔎 Marks search results from human-made sites on Folkweb’s boost list or yours, and hides sites you block, in Google, DuckDuckGo and Bing.
  • 🟢 Reads human.json: sites saying a person runs them, and vouching for others you can choose to trust.
  • 📷 Checks Content Credentials (C2PA) on any image when you ask: who signed it and how it was made, when that information exists.
  • 🌿 No autoplay, still pages, and optional retro looks for every site: Newsprint, Terminal, ’98 and 2000s Web 2.0.

What it deliberately doesn’t do

  • 🚫 It doesn’t guess whether something is “AI”. No tool can do that honestly. Missing signals mean “unknown”, never “fake”.
  • 🔒 It doesn’t track you. No account, no servers, no analytics. Your settings and lists stay in your browser.
  • 🍪 It doesn’t block trackers or cookie banners. Keep your browser’s tracking protection or uBlock Origin; Folkweb works alongside them.
  • 👀 It never hides things silently. One click shows everything it hid on a page.

Principles

  1. Evidence over guesses. Show why, never a black-box score.
  2. People over algorithms. You choose your curators and your circle.
  3. Calm by default. Less motion, fewer counts, no autoplay.
  4. Private by design. No data leaves your browser except what you ask for.
  5. Built on open standards. human.json, C2PA, RSS, webrings, uBlock list syntax.
  6. AI that humanizes. AI should help people find and understand each other’s work, on your terms: optional, clearly labelled and private. People decide. See our AI policy.

AI that works for people

Today: you decide which AI features you see on the sites you use. Folkweb can hide AI panels and summaries you didn’t ask for, and Google can open as plain web results. Everything can be switched back on.

Exploring next: optional helpers that use AI to bring you closer to people, not further away. For example: plain-language headlines instead of clickbait, short summaries that point you to the original author, translation so you can read small personal sites in other languages, and help finding the person behind an article.

The rules: off until you turn them on, clearly labelled, running on your own device where possible, never sending your browsing to us, and never used to guess whether something was made by a person.

Run a website? Help people find it

Publish a small human.json file to say a person is behind your site, and vouch for sites you know are made by people. Folkweb and other tools can read it.

{
  "version": "0.1.1",
  "url": "https://your-site.example",
  "vouches": [
    { "url": "https://a-friend.example", "vouched_at": "2026-10-12" }
  ]
}

Then link it from your pages: <link rel="human-json" href="/human.json">. This site does the same — see ours. You can also add an /ai page saying how you use AI.
